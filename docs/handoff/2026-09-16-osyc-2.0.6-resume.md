## Release decision

Do not deploy the candidate, restart services, ~~publish a prerelease~~ (the plug-in prerelease clause was overridden on 2026-09-16 for mobile UI validation — see the status update above), or promote a stable `2.0.6` until the synchronised backend has:

1. the diagnostic and email contracts;
2. the `2.0.6` runtime release ID and `>=2.0.3 <2.1.0` compatibility range;
3. a green complete backend suite, including the quota-refund regression;
4. health, runtime-info, and zero-cost dry-run evidence.

Keep `2.0.5` and `/srv/osyc/backups/20260915-stable205-pre` as the rollback path.
